I am a research engineer with an M.S. in Artificial Intelligence from Rochester Institute of Technology. My work focuses on responsible AI, socially aligned NLP, LLM evals, and large-scale data. I built an LLM-as-judge pipeline that analyzed 6.7M parliamentary speeches and produced agreement with human experts. In industry, I developed end-to-end ML pipelines that improved model stability, reduced processing time, and supported high-volume enterprise workflows. I have also published at EMNLP twice and mentored interns in feature engineering, PySpark, and ML evaluation.
